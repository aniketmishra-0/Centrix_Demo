📄 Specification & Architecture Index

Directly Indexed From 11 Specification Docs (.md)

Centrix is engineered under mathematical matching formulas, edge-first architecture, and enterprise Windows security models. Click any specification below to interrogate its contents with our AI assistant.

MATCHING_ENGINE_SPEC.md Core Engine

7-Signal Deterministic Matching Engine

Deterministic weighted scoring formula: Room (15%), Time Overlap (35%), Duration (15%), Teacher Roster (15%), Batch/Subject (10%), and Historical Drift (10%). Auto-assigns at ≥85% confidence.

ARCHITECTURE.md System Design

Edge-First + Serverless Architecture

FileSystemWatcher with 10-second stability lock prevents incomplete uploads. Video bytes stream directly from classroom PCs to Google Drive in 10MB chunks with zero intermediate transcoding servers.

DATABASE_SCHEMA.md Durable Storage

SQLite 3 WAL & Sync Architecture

Durable tables for devices, lecture_sessions, timetable_cache, upload_queue, and outbox_events. Write-Ahead Logging guarantees zero corruption during sudden classroom power blackouts.

SECURITY_MODEL.md Zero Trust

Windows DPAPI & 4-Tier RBAC

OS-level DPAPI encryption locks Google OAuth refresh tokens to specific machines. 4-Tier RBAC matrix segregates Super Admin, Center Admin, Reviewer, and Room Operator permissions.

STATE_MACHINES.md Lifecycle

Lecture Lifecycle & Retry Backoff

Audited transitions: DETECTED → PROCESSING → AUTO_ASSIGNED / REVIEW_REQUIRED → CONFIRMED → UPLOADING → VERIFIED (SHA-256 hash matched). Exponential backoff handles network drops.

COST_CONTROL.md FinOps Scale

Abhi Ke Kharch Se Kafi Kam

Centrix is engineered to operate at a tiny fraction of current center expenses. By leveraging edge classroom PCs and direct Drive uploads, heavy cloud video transcode fleets and manual pen drive logistics costs are completely eliminated.
